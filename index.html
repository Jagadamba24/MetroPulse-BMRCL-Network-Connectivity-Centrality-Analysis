📐 Graph Theory Formulations

Degree Centrality: C_D(v) = deg(v) / (N - 1)

Measures direct track branching. Majestic = 4; regular pass-through stations = 2; terminals = 1.

Betweenness Centrality: C_B(v) = Σ (σ_st(v) / σ_st)

Fraction of shortest paths passing through station v. Brandes algorithm.

Closeness Centrality: C_C(v) = (N - 1) / Σ d(v, u)

Inverse of sum of shortest path distances from v to all other stations.

Transitivity: T = (3 × Triangles) / Triads = 0.0000

Strictly zero because Namma Metro is a planar tree graph with zero triangular loops.

Reciprocity: r = |E_directed_bidirectional| / |E_total| = 1.0000

Commercial metro lines have dual tracks running both directions.
